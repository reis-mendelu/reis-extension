Study rooms on the map

Phone and iPad only. The app asks MENDELU's Bookings directly, with no reIS server in between. Czech copy, made-up student. Tap targets are 44px or more. Room shapes are the real polygons from the campus map data (building A, floor −1).

Green = free team room, blue = free individual room, grey = taken, dark outline = chosen.

The main path: pin, room, confirm

0: a small pin on the campus overview, the same disc as an event. B: tap it and the list opens with every room's next free hour and its own button; the IC rooms are further down the list, a tap on one flies there. C: tap a room on the map for other hours. D and E: confirm and done.

0 · Opening the map. The same small white disc the events use, carrying the library glyph and a green count of free rooms. No label, no bar, nothing new over the map: the overview stays as crowded as it is today. It is the old PR\'s pin (LibraryLayer), reused. One tap flies to building A and opens the list (frame B).
