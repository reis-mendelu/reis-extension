# Botanical garden photo bubbles — design

Date: 2026-09-19
Status: approved, ready for an implementation plan
Canvas of the approved option: https://claude.ai/artifact/1QiMY7b574o2pXLo9vHH5h


> **Superseded in part, 2026-09-20.** What shipped differs from this spec on
> four points, each decided while building and verified in the browser:
>
> 1. **The card is the photograph and nothing else.** No name, no section, no
>    `why` line, no opening hours. A caption under a picture is the part nobody
>    reads; the name survives as the marker tooltip and the image's alt text.
>    Pressing the photo maximizes it full-screen (portalled to `<body>`, because
>    the map screen's `isolate` traps z-index).
> 2. **Both image sizes are bundled**, not fetched from jsDelivr. At four places
>    that is ~930 KB and buys offline-in-the-garden with no cache, no
>    `DB_VERSION` bump and no content hash. **Revisit past a dozen places** —
>    the CDN reasoning below becomes right again.
> 3. **There is no drill-in gate**, because `test` removed drill-in entirely
>    (the garden is scenery you walk through). The only gate is zoom, and the
>    threshold is viewport-relative: `map.getBoundsZoom(gardenBounds, false,
>    L.point(50, 50))`, matching the padding at `MapCanvas.tsx:289`. Two fixed
>    floors shipped-and-failed first — 17 hid the bubbles the instant the garden
>    opened, then 16 did the same on a 375px phone.
> 4. **`number`, `section` and `why` are optional**, and a place without a photo
>    draws *nothing* rather than a dot. The best places — the ponds, the little
>    wood — are not on the official plan at all.
>
> Coordinates came from georeferencing a hand-annotated screenshot of this same
> basemap against the garden polygon's extremes, not from the official plan
> image, which never resolved. Every point is inside the garden and 7–19 m from
> a mapped footpath.
>
> **Also corrected:** this spec says "There is no lake." That was wrong — it
> concluded from OSM's silence. The garden has ponds (*jezírka*), and they are
> one of the four places that shipped.

## The idea

The MENDELU botanical garden and arboretum is 11 hectares of places students
never see, 300 m from the campus they sit in every day. reIS already draws the
garden — its OSM outline, its 32 footpaths — but the inside is two unlabelled
dots.

A **photo bubble** is a small circular crop of a real photograph, pinned at a
place on the garden's inner map. At rest it is 28 px: you can tell it is a
photograph and nothing more. Under the cursor it grows to 56 px and names
itself. Clicked, it opens into a card with the photograph, the place, one line
saying why you would walk there, and the opening hours.

The curation line, which decides every `why` line and every photo: **places you
would send a friend to sit.** Not the prettiest specimens and not a taxonomy —
somewhere to spend twenty minutes.

## Scope

- **31 places** get coordinates, matching the garden's own published plan.
- **12 of them** get a photograph and a `why` line and render as bubbles.
- **19 of them** stay plain dots that still open a card with name and section.
- The garden only. Other remote places (Lednice, Žabčice, Křtiny, Panská lícha)
  are untouched.
- A guided walking order over these places (option C on the canvas) is
  **deliberately deferred**. It reuses this data unchanged — an `order` array
  over the same ids — and is not designed here.

## What the research established

Sources: `arboretum.mendelu.cz` (Mapa areálu, Otevírací doba), Overpass over the
garden polygon.

- The garden publishes a **numbered plan of 31 named places in 5 thematic
  sections** (1.1 Správní budova … 5.14 Růže). That is the inventory; it does
  not need inventing.
- **OpenStreetMap knows almost nothing inside the fence.** Overpass over the
  `area` ring of place `-101` returns 15 elements: 2 greenhouses, 2 unnamed
  viewpoints, a firepit, 2 gates, and unnamed buildings. No named collection, no
  water. **Coordinates cannot be scraped** — they are produced by georeferencing
  the plan image against the 32 footpaths already in `remotePlaces.json`.
- **There is no lake.** The garden's water feature is the *Vodní kaskáda* (3.2).
- **MENDELU students and staff enter free with the university card** (others pay
  150 Kč). Regular hours are **Po–Pá 7:00–15:00, closed weekends and holidays**;
  extended to 9:00–18:00 including weekends during events. The gate needs the
  buzzer. This is the single most useful fact the feature can carry, and the
  reason the hours belong on the card: a beautiful photo of somewhere closed
  today is a letdown.

## Data

Two files. **A place appears in exactly one of them** — enforced by test.

### `src/data/map/gardenPlaces.json` (new, hand-authored)

The twelve. Never generated by any script.

```ts
interface GardenPlace {
  id: string;          // "rokle" — stable, and the photo filename stem
  number: string;      // "2.6" — the official plan's numbering
  section: 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5;
  name: { cz: string; en: string };
  why: { cz: string; en: string };   // one line: the reason to walk there
  lon: number;
  lat: number;
  photo?: string;      // the FULL photo's hashed filename on the CDN, e.g. "rokle.8f3a1c.webp"
  credit?: string;     // author + licence; rendered only when set
}
```

`section` numbers the five published parts (1 Okolí správní budovy, 2 Jižní
svahy, 3 Centrální část, 4 Staré arboretum, 5 Botanický systém). The section
name itself is a translated UI string, not data.

**`photo` is optional on purpose.** Coordinates are one georeferencing pass and
can land first; photographs are handpicked on their own schedule. A place with no
`photo` yet renders as a plain dot and opens a card without an image, so the
feature ships incrementally and never blocks on a photo that has not been chosen.
A place is a *bubble* exactly when it has a thumb.

### `remotePlaces.json` → `places[-101].pois` (existing)

The other nineteen, in the shape this array already has (`{ name, lon, lat }`).
`fetch-remote-places.mjs:176` lists `pois` in `PRESERVE`, so hand-curated points
already survive a regeneration — no change to that script is needed.

**They do not exist yet.** `pois` holds two entries today. After `Skleníky`
moves out, the georeferencing pass authors **18 new `pois` entries** alongside the
12 `gardenPlaces` entries. The count reconciles as 31 = 12 bubbles + 19 dots,
where the 19 are `Správní budova` (1.1, already present) plus the 18 new ones.

`Skleníky` is a `poi` today and is expected to be one of the twelve: it **moves**
into `gardenPlaces.json` rather than being duplicated. `Správní budova` is a
building, not a place to sit, and stays a dot.

`gardenPlaces.json` is kept out of `remotePlaces.json` because that file is
generated geometry imported wholesale by `mapLayers.ts` on every map view, and
this is hand-written editorial content with a different lifecycle.

## Photos

Two sizes, two homes, because the closed bubble and the open card have opposite
requirements.

| | size | where | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| thumb | 96×96 webp, ~4 KB | **bundled**, `public/garden/<id>.webp` | the state you see 95 % of the time; must paint instantly, offline, with no network |
| full | 800×480 webp, ~70 KB | **reis-data → jsDelivr**, fetched on expand, cached in IndexedDB | 12 × 70 KB ≈ 850 KB is too much for every store build and both native apps |

Total bundle cost: ~50 KB. The thumb is addressed by id (`public/garden/<id>.webp`) because it ships with
the build and cannot go stale. The full-photo client copies
`src/api/successRate.ts` (`cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/reis-mendelu/reis-data@main` +
IDB cache) so nothing new is invented. It talks to no Supabase table and sends
nothing about the student — it is a `GET` of a static file.

**Offline behaviour.** Signal in the garden is unreliable. The card opens
immediately with the 96 px thumb scaled up and blurred, and swaps in the full
photo when it arrives. Never a grey box, never a bare spinner. A failed fetch
leaves the blurred thumb in place and logs through `logError('Api.fetchGardenPhoto', …)`.

**Cache invalidation.** jsDelivr caches `@main` mutably, so *replacing* a photo
at the same path will not propagate promptly. The full photo's filename therefore carries a
short content hash (`rokle.8f3a1c.webp`) and is recorded in the `photo` field; a
replaced photo is a new filename and a one-line data change. This is the one operational cost
of hosting the full photos off-bundle, and it is accepted.

**Provenance.** Photos are handpicked by the maintainer. Anything sourced from
`arboretum.mendelu.cz` is MENDELU's and needs permission before it ships;
Wikimedia Commons holds 44 freely-licensed photographs of this garden whose terms
are per-file. `credit` carries author and licence and renders in small type under
the photo whenever it is set, and is empty for the maintainer's own photographs.

## Rendering

### Layer

New `src/components/CampusMap/gardenBubbleLayer.ts`. Each bubble is an
`L.divIcon` wrapping an `<img>` of the bundled thumb, given the class
`garden-bubble`. Styling lives in `src/index.css` beside `.room-label` and
`.place-label` — Leaflet builds this DOM and takes a single `className`, which is
the standing exception to the no-custom-CSS rule.

The layer is gated by the **existing drill-in rule** (`paths`/`pois` are revealed
only when the site is opened) plus a zoom floor, so nothing changes at campus
overview and the garden does not become a wall of photographs seen from orbit.

### Desktop (`src/components/CampusMap/`)

Rest 28 px. `:hover` scales to 56 px with a CSS transform — no JS, no React state
per marker. The name appears in a `place-label` tooltip. Click opens the existing
detail panel with a photo header above the current content.

### Mobile (`src/components/mobile/screens/map/`)

A separate tree, and there is **no hover**. The middle state does not exist:
bubbles rest at 44 px (the touch-target minimum) and one tap opens `MapSheet`
directly. The sheet renders a selection at `h-auto max-h-[70vh]`, so it hugs its
content and a photo header does not force an aspect ratio or risk the `#root`
clip.

### Selection

A new `MapSelection` kind, `{ kind: 'gardenPlace'; place: GardenPlace }`,
threaded through `MapSidePanel` (desktop) and `MapPanelBody` (mobile). The
nineteen plain dots continue to resolve to the existing `poi` selection.

Both cards show: the photo, the name, the section and official number, the `why`
line, and the hours strip — *Po–Pá 7:00–15:00 · zdarma s průkazem MENDELU*.

### Language

`name` and `why` are stored `{ cz, en }` and read with the app's `'cz' | 'en'`
code directly, unmapped. Section names and the hours strip are `useTranslation()`
strings in `src/i18n/locales/{cs,en}.json`.

## Testing

Failing test first, per the project's Iron Rules.

1. **`gardenPlaces.json` schema** — unique ids; `name.cz`, `name.en`, `why.cz`,
   `why.en` all non-empty; `number` matches the official plan's pattern;
   `section` in 1–5.
2. **Geometry** — every `lon`/`lat` in both files falls **inside the garden
   polygon**, by point-in-polygon against `places[-101].area` in
   `remotePlaces.json`. This is the test that catches a georeferencing slip.
3. **No duplication** — no id or name appears in both `gardenPlaces.json` and
   `pois`.
4. **Assets** — a place with a `photo` has `public/garden/<id>.webp`, and every
   `photo` value is a hashed filename, so a replaced photo cannot silently reuse
   a URL. A place with neither still renders, as a dot.
5. **Count** — `gardenPlaces.json` plus `pois` together cover 31 places with no
   gap in the official numbering.
6. **Photo client** — cache hit returns without network; cache miss fetches once;
   a CDN failure resolves without throwing and leaves the card usable.
7. **Laziness guard** — no photo request is issued at startup or on map open;
   only on expand. (Fetch-once-at-startup is stale forever under Capacitor.)
8. **Layer** — one marker per place when drilled in and above the zoom floor;
   none when either condition fails.
9. **`verify-ui`** at 320 / 390 / 430 before the work is called done, with the
   overflow, collision and contrast assertions. A bubble over a path label and
   white-on-photo text are the two likely findings.
